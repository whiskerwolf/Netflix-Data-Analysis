# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Name
**Netflix Data Analysis**

## 2. Product Overview
Netflix Data Analysis is a Python-based Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project that analyzes the Netflix Titles dataset to identify patterns in content type, countries, release years, ratings, and genres.

## 3. Objective
The objective is to transform the Netflix Titles dataset into useful visual and statistical insights that help understand the composition and growth of Netflix's content catalog.

## 4. Dataset
- **Dataset:** Netflix Titles Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Rows:** 8,807
- **Columns:** 12

## 5. Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## 6. Functional Requirements
The project should:
1. Load the Netflix dataset.
2. Inspect the dataset structure and missing values.
3. Perform required data cleaning.
4. Analyze Movies vs TV Shows.
5. Analyze countries producing Netflix content.
6. Analyze content released over the years.
7. Analyze content ratings.
8. Analyze the most common genres.
9. Generate visualizations for the major analyses.
10. Present key findings in an understandable format.

## 7. Visualizations
The project includes:
- Movies vs TV Shows
- Top 10 Countries Producing Netflix Content
- Netflix Content Released Over the Years
- Netflix Content by Rating
- Top 10 Genres on Netflix

## 8. Key Insights
- Movies dominate the Netflix catalog, with more than 6,100 titles, while TV Shows account for around 2,700.
- The United States contributes the highest number of Netflix titles, followed by India.
- Content releases increased rapidly after 2010 and reached the highest level around 2020.
- TV-MA and TV-14 are the most common ratings.
- International Movies is the most common genre, followed by Dramas and Comedies.

## 9. Future Improvements
- Deeper genre analysis
- Expanded rating analysis
- Duration analysis

## 10. Success Criteria
The project is considered complete when the dataset is successfully analyzed, the required visualizations are generated, and the major findings are documented clearly.
